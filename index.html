FAQ

Common Questions

Do you take on commissioned choreography?
Yes. I am available for commissions for dance companies, film, theatre, and interdisciplinary projects. Please reach out via email with details about your project, timeline, and budget.
Do you teach workshops or masterclasses?
I offer workshops in contemporary choreography, movement improvisation, and dance film. Workshops can be tailored for students, professional dancers, or community groups.
Are you available for international projects?
Yes. I am open to international collaborations and have experience working across different cultural contexts and time zones.
